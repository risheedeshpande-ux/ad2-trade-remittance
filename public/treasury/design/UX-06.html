State coverage matrix

Derived from the build  ·  79 components carry all four states  ·  1 screen is static by nature

Design noteGenerated from the registered screens at build time, not written by hand, so it cannot drift from what was actually built. Every data-bearing component declares what its empty, loading, error and partial states say. Partial is the one most often skipped and the one that matters most here: FX Plus arrives in batches and an aggregator can be stale, so a screen showing some of the truth must say so rather than looking complete.

1Static screens — no states required

0Undeclared
The rule partial exists for Never show part of the truth as though it were all of it. If a feed is mid-batch or a source is stale, the figure stays visible, says how old it is, and is excluded from any "fresh" subtotal with the exclusion stated on screen.
