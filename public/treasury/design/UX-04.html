45 dialogs  ·  every one is live on this page  ·  click to open

Design noteEvery actionable control in the prototype resolves to one of these. Open any of them here rather than hunting for the screen that triggers it. Destructive actions name exactly what happens and to how many records; anything that returns work to a colleague requires a reason that is carried back to them.

Rules these dialogs enforce No silent destruction — a destructive dialog names the record count and the exact effect. Reason mandatory on reject, cancel and adjust; it is surfaced back to the maker. A checker never sees an enabled approve button on their own entry. Suggestions never auto-commit; the confirm step is always a human action.
