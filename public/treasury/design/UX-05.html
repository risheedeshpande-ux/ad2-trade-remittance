Environment, freshness and role states

Production successors to the prototype chip  ·  staleness  ·  read-only

Design noteThree system-wide patterns that must be settled before anything ships. The environment chip is the one that matters most: nobody should ever be unable to tell a training environment from production.

1 · Environment signalling

The prototype's “Illustrative data” chip needs production successors. Colour alone never carries this — each is a word.

2 · Freshness and staleness

Any figure sourced from FX Plus, the aggregator, Euronet, Pine Labs or the IBR provider carries its sync time. Treasury must never act on a number without knowing its age.

StateIndicatorThresholdBehaviour
FreshFX Plus 14:32Within expected intervalIncluded in subtotals
AgeingAggregator 13:05Past one refresh cycleIncluded, flagged
StaleManual 10 Sep — stalePast the agreed thresholdVisible, excluded from the fresh subtotal, and the exclusion is stated
Never hide a stale figureDropping it silently from a subtotal is the failure mode this pattern exists to prevent. Show it, mark it, and say it was excluded.
