Offline capture

Use only when the portal cannot reach Treasury  ·  0 pending entries

Design noteIf the portal is unavailable, a deal taken at the counter still creates an exposure. Capturing it here keeps it from becoming an unhedged sale discovered days later.

Retrospective deal entry

Available only when the portal is offline or Treasury is unreachable. Entries are queued locally and submitted automatically on reconnect. Each one is flagged for treasury review because the rate was committed outside the system.

Whether the counter may continue selling during an outage is an EBIXCASH policy decision — PRD Q20. This screen assumes yes, with mandatory retrospective capture.
