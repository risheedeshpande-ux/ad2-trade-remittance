Cut-off and cancellation queue

Pending transaction links  ·  Cut-off 16:00 IST  ·  45 minutes remaining

Design noteReminders expose the residual only. Transaction and cover status are rechecked at cut-off. Cancellation releases exposure, and the cover impact routes to Treasury. Every row offers a recovery path: a position the branch simply failed to tag can be requoted or linked, while a position whose deal died can only be cancelled. The two failures are labelled differently because they need different follow-ups.
