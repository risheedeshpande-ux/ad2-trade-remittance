1 · Architecture

Two portals render from one in-memory object persisted to localStorage. There is no server: an AD2 action mutates the same object the SME portal reads, which is why approvals and allocations appear instantly on the other side.

A production build replaces the bottom band with real integrations and moves the access gate server-side; the middle two bands are the parts that carry over as-is.

2 · End-to-end journey

The full arc from a business that has never transacted to a settled bill. Each column is a stage; the lane shows who is acting.

Stage 3 is upstream of stage 5: nothing can be knocked off that was never lodged, which is why bill lodgement is its own module rather than a step inside a transaction.

4 · Outward remittance flow

The longest journey in the build, and the one carrying the two decisions from the requirements call: purpose-code-driven documents, and a rate that the customer books rather than requests.

The two amber steps are where the requirements call changed the BRD. Everything downstream of the submission gate is unchanged workflow.

5 · Inward remittance and disposal

Deliberately shorter than outward. The call cut the disposal page to two decisions and removed trade-finance disposal entirely.

The available balance is the control: total disposal across all instructions can never exceed the receipt.

6 · Bill lodgement

Four capture channels converge on one register. Review status and settlement status are tracked independently — a bill can be accepted and still unsettled.

Outstanding amount on this register is the number the knock-off screen works against — which is why lodgement is upstream of everything.

7 · Knock-off mechanic

Which remittances are eligible against which bills, and where a balance actually moves.

Suggest Matches proposes on currency, counterparty, reference and amount. It never commits: the user confirms, then AD2 confirms again before any balance moves.

8 · Assisted journey

AD2 picks an SME up from the directory and works inside that SME's portal on its behalf. No separate screens: the same SME portal is reused with the acting context swapped, which is why anything the SME can do, AD2 can do for them.

The audit trail names both parties on every assisted action — "submitted … (assisted, on behalf of Meridian Textiles)" — so the record is never ambiguous about who acted.

9 · Status machines

Four independent status flows. The bill's two are genuinely independent — that is a requirement, not an accident.

Dashed arrows are return loops. Every rejection and every clarification requires a typed reason in the build.

10 · Data model

Eleven entities. The two that carry the money logic are Bill and Allocation — everything else describes, permits or records.

Outstanding balances are never stored as an independent truth — they are derived from the bill's original amount less confirmed allocations, which is what keeps the two settlement paths consistent.
Provisional: the purpose-code to document mapping shown in the outward flow covers six outward and four inward codes and stands in for the business team's full workbook. It is the fix for the defect where every purpose code served the same checklist, but it is not the final list.
