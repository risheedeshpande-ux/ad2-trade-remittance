Worth trying

Five things that show what this build is actually for.

WhereWhat to do
Outward → New request Pick a remittance type and watch the purpose codes narrow. Switch the purpose between Business travel and Payment towards imports — the document checklist swaps, so travel never asks for a Bill of Entry. That was the defect raised on the requirements call.
Outward → New request Book rate locks the customer rate for 48 hours and notifies treasury with no bank approval step. Enter FX deal reference is the alternative for a rate negotiated by phone. Submission is blocked until one of the two exists.
Inward → Dispose Two decisions only: the export bill to knock off and the account to credit. Apply part of the receipt and the balance stays available for a later instruction.
AD2 → SME Directory Transact on behalf puts an AD2 user inside that SME's portal. Anything submitted is stamped as assisted and the audit names both parties — but the SME's own controls still apply.
AD2 → Applications Approve an application: set permission, currencies, margins and charges. Enter a USD margin and leave the rest blank to see them derive from the currency multiplier. Then switch to that SME and the pricing is live.

Resetting

State is held in your browser's localStorage, so anything you do persists between visits and is yours alone — nothing is sent anywhere. Reset demo data in the yellow banner restores the original seed at any time.

Scope

This is a frontend prototype for review, not a system. A production build still needs the full purpose-code workbook, the FX quotation direction and pricing formula, transaction limits, the SFTP and SAP integration contracts, and backend enforcement of authentication, authorisation and validation. Those open items are listed in the PRD.
